# Standards adaptation grant

Grantor: International Deaf Emergency, Inc. (IDE), a Deaf-led United States
501(c)(3) nonprofit, publisher of the Three Senses Alerting Standard.

Effective for every published draft of the standard from 0.6.0 onward.

## The grant

IDE grants to the United States Federal Communications Commission, to the
Federal Emergency Management Agency, and to any recognized standards
development organization (including, without limitation, ATIS, the 3GPP
organizational partners, OASIS, ETSI, ISO, IEC, and ITU) a perpetual,
worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to reproduce,
adapt, translate, create derivative works from, and incorporate the
normative material of the Three Senses Alerting Standard, namely:

1. the machine-readable vocabulary (`vocabulary.json`),
2. the conformance requirements and test vectors (`conformance/`), and
3. the specification text (`THE-STANDARD.md`),

into public standards, technical reports, rules, and regulations, with
attribution to International Deaf Emergency.

The no-derivatives condition of the CC BY-ND 4.0 license on the
specification text does not apply to uses within this grant.

## What the grant does not cover

The names "Three Senses Alerting Standard" and "Alerts for All" and any
conformance mark identify material as published by IDE. A derived or
modified vocabulary must not be presented under those names. This protects
users, not IDE: no one may change what FIRE feels like and still call it
Three Senses.
